Concrete change
NpImuTimeCorrection.cs adds a streaming distinct-valid-sample journal and a
file-only converter. V1 NpUsbSensors.Feed/FeedSnapshot and V2's verified sensor
header consumer opt in during live neural USB recording when misc saving is on.
After sensor files close and stopped clock queries are saved, correction writes
a measured-values CSV, one float64 PC Unix-second timestamp per retained sample
row, validity/bounds, source/output hashes and a calibration report. No samples
are reconstructed from the1250Hz held analogin grid. Duplicate/invalid records
do not become new sample rows. Missing observations are not filled.

Raw native counter, MCU IRQ-completion ms, sequence, available firmware session/
transport metadata, validity, full raw snapshot and actual host receipt QPC are
retained. The sensor counter unwraps only for consistent short forward progress;
resets/ambiguous wraps/gaps are labeled. MCU reset rows remain unbound to old
clock observations. No clock calls/USB queries are introduced by this converter.
The host receipt is provenance, not substituted for sample time.

Reference and uncertainty
This maps the MCU I2C-completion proxy to PC time, using bracketed control queries
with integer-ms phase and epoch uncertainty. It does not establish physical ADC,
read-latch-to-completion, filter/update, IRQ latency, clock nonlinearity, lossless
200Hz capture or a1ms NP/GPIO bound. The neural67–135us first-row intervals are
not assigned to IMU samples. Old firmware works with available coarse evidence;
missing identity/clock coverage leaves NaN PC time without gating neural Start.

Limits and next milestone
V2's device I/O acquisition fault is not fixed by a host converter; firmware
diagnosis belongs to the V2 owner. Valid advancing acquisition under simultaneous
NP load remains unverified with128. Existing V1 HIL files retain a held grid,
not their discarded native samples, so they cannot validate native replay.
This first milestone covers live USB neural recording and retained raw replay;
it does not add native BLE/SD sample transport or reconstruct absent samples.

The concrete bounded joint test is prepared in docs/imu-time-correction.md:
exact identities/hashes, one WILDX process, existing GPIO wiring only,70seconds
per NP device sequentially (V1P12/native15k, V2P123/20k), source/query/event
retention, validity/row/gap/overflow/epoch criteria, explicit Stop, and full
original-image restoration by the firmware owners if temporary USB images are
authorized. GPIO's exact installed identity and Frank's direct hardware approval
are required before execution. No events means no event-timing qualification;
no synthetic pulses or physical1ms claim is inferred.
