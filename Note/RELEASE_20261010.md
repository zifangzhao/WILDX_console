## Compatible firmware and limits

The engineering NP2 logger in zifangzhao/XE_NP, branch V2, LATEST_FIRMWARE/V2_NP2 retains loader97C3716A / USB DFB8F25E / APP ED881256. Its HEX/container identities are pinned in [the manifest](../LATEST_INSTALLERS/release-manifest.json); no firmware is bundled or flashed by this installer. That resident enables P1+P2 only: logical P3/SPI2/D8 is disabled. Self-test actions separately require compatible newer resident firmware such as the private C3C76C43 service; DFB8F25E does not implement those diagnostic requests.

Retained APP dual20 native readback passed31.50336s; dual30 failed at815.6128s with source retention overflow. A separate10-second generated CLI USB test after the user's cable change passed46.14MB/s payload without sequence gaps/mismatches, but its pending-TX stop needed a restart. Neither qualifies GUI acquisition, lossless endurance or analog quality.

No USB/BLE/SWD hardware operation, firmware build/flash/reset, card access or new recording was performed for this release. Live GUI capture/control/update, ROM DFU, clean install/uninstall and full data quality remain open. This is an offline-compatible, software-verified engineering release, **not** a newly end-to-end-qualified firmware/installer pair.
