# V1 USB SD200mA policy and U5 fallback correction

The USB-only correction explicitly requests group4/function0 for SDR104,
SDR50 and the SDR25/SDR12 HAL paths. A selected UHS mode, including SDR25,
must report support and selection of function0. This is a card power allowance,
not a measured200mA clamp. The SD/BLE recording APP and root V2 are unchanged.

## Reproduced defect

On the mapped V1 UID001D00065942500B20313354/ST-Link3439, a one-shot automatically
resumed breakpoint captured16valid CMD6 words, selected HIGH function1, and
SDMMC STA00000100(DATAEND). RXFIFOE was clear after DPSM retirement. The strict
checker incorrectly rejected that valid completion because of its extra idle
RXFIFOE gate. This is not evidence of a legacy HAL leaving unread FIFO tails.
The corrected reader admits two eight-word bursts only on positiveRXFIFOHF,
then requires exact16words, DATAEND and no CRC/timeout/overrun. No idle FIFO
flush or SD write is introduced. CMD6/allF remains a read-only current-selection
query. Fresh-cold DEFAULT still requires repeated reference reads but no
optional CMD6, including on older cards.

The first200mA-only image retained the defect: reset succeeded after24.048s,
and two1GiB known-hash reads were clean at9.69MB/s in DEFAULT. A test-adapter
epoch mismatch after its manually forced reset was corrected by reopening the
production stream; no host product change was needed. The next checker fix
passed two identical1GiB reads at14.51MB/s. Its HIGH path still preserved a
prior card allowance, so the final HAL also explicitly selects0 for SDR25.

## Limits

The local card selects30MHz SDR25 fallback, approximately14.5MB/s, not the
previous43MB/s UHS result. Cold initialization/reset takes about23seconds;
an early format-capability read during initial preparation returned zeroed
data, so those early preflight failures are retained separately, not counted
as download passes. The inaccessible remote card and analog rail current/
droop have not been measured. No claim that power alone caused its failure.

No new streaming capture, SD/BLE recording, camera, endurance or card-removal
qualification. Bundled APP654824DE is unchanged and differs from the private
bench APP; this is bounded USB download validation, not a fresh whole-pair HIL
pass. Installer runtime remains51; GUI55 and V2/camera targets are excluded.
